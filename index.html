Data quality audit

Validation workflow

Every record in the Fitbit dataset runs through the same checks before it reaches a chart. Clean records go straight to reporting, questionable ones are held for review, and days with no real activity data are excluded so they don't drag down the averages.

  • 674 validated 72%
  • 194 held for review 21%
  • 72 excluded 8%
